About Eventistry

Event styling with a personal touch

Private celebrations and corporate events across the UAE.

How we work

Eventistry is a UAE-based event decoration and styling team. We work with families and corporate clients on everything from intimate proposals and children’s parties to seasonal installations and company events.

We bring together backdrops, balloons, florals, lighting and display details so the finished setup feels cohesive. We start with your venue and inspiration, then work with you on the palette, focal points and installation requirements.

For private events, we plan focal points such as a cake display or photo backdrop around the room and guest flow. For offices and corporate events, we work with the brief, venue restrictions and any brand colours you supply.

Planning & installation

From first brief to event day

  1. Share your brief

    Tell us who you’re celebrating, where the event takes place and what you want guests to see first. Photos of the venue and any colour references help.

  2. Review the styling plan

    We’ll discuss focal points, colours and décor elements, then confirm the proposed scope and quotation.

  3. Coordinate the installation

    Ahead of the event, we’ll confirm access times, setup space and any collection arrangements included in the quotation.

Details that shape the setup

Designed for your venue

Ceiling height, available floor space and the setup window all affect what can be installed. Photos of your venue help us suggest proportions, placement and materials suited to the space.

Share any access rules early so we can include them in the plan and quote.
