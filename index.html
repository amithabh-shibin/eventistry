Frequently asked questions

Find out what to send us and what goes into a quote.

Where do you provide event decoration?

We style events in Dubai and across the UAE. Include your venue or area when you request a quote so we can confirm availability and setup details.

What should I include in my quotation request?

Share the date, location, occasion, estimated budget and any colours or themes you have in mind. You can attach reference photos to the form or share them on WhatsApp.

Can I adapt a package to my theme?

Yes. Tell us which package you like and which elements you want to add or change. We’ll confirm the scope and price in your quotation.

How much does event decoration cost?

The gallery lists celebration package starting prices. The final quote depends on the venue, installation size, materials, custom details and access requirements.

When should I book?

Get in touch once you know your date and location. For a last-minute booking, call or WhatsApp us so we can check availability.

Do you work with my venue’s setup rules?

Share the venue’s access times, loading instructions and décor restrictions. We’ll discuss the setup plan and any collection requirements as part of your quote.

Share your date, venue and ideas for a tailored quotation.
